How does the trial work?

Everything is unlocked for 7 days, free. After that you buy BOMBOCUT once to keep editing. Nothing renews and nothing is charged automatically. Your projects stay on your iPhone either way.

I bought it, but it asks me to pay again

Open About in the app and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

The subtitle download fails or is slow

The speech model is about 650 MB and downloads once. Use Wi-Fi, keep the app open until it finishes, and make sure your iPhone has free space. After that, subtitles work fully offline.
