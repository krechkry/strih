BOMBOCUT Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026 · Support · Česky

Short version: BOMBOCUT collects no data. Your videos, captions and projects stay on your iPhone. There is no account, no cloud, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Who we are

BOMBOCUT is made by Kryštof Krechler, a self-employed filmmaker in Prague, Czech Republic. Contact: bombocut.support@gmail.com.

What happens on your iPhone

All editing, speech recognition (automatic subtitles), caption translation, noise removal, subject masks, tracking and export run on your device. Nothing you edit is uploaded anywhere. Projects are stored in the app's own storage on your iPhone and are removed when you delete them or the app.

Photos

BOMBOCUT uses the system Photos picker, so it only sees the videos and pictures you pick. When you save an export, it asks for permission to add to your library and never reads it.

When the app uses the internet

  • Speech model: downloaded once from Hugging Face when you set up subtitles.
  • Translation models: downloaded from Mozilla's servers for the caption languages you pick.
  • Purchases: the free trial and the one-time purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

These downloads are ordinary file requests. Like any website, those servers receive your IP address to deliver the file, under their own privacy policies (Hugging Face, Mozilla, Apple). No information about you or your videos is sent with them.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no selling or sharing of data.

Feedback emails

If you email us (for example with the Send feedback button), we receive your email address, your message and the app and iOS version the button adds. We use them only to reply and to improve the app, and delete the conversation when it's no longer needed. You can ask us to delete it any time.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what we hold about you, and have it corrected or deleted. Since the app collects nothing, this only concerns emails you have sent us. You can also complain to the Czech data protection authority (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, uoou.gov.cz).

Children

BOMBOCUT collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Zásady ochrany soukromí

BOMBOCUT nesbírá žádná data. Videa, titulky a projekty zůstávají v tvém iPhonu. Žádný účet, žádný cloud, žádná analytika, reklamy ani sledování. Veškeré úpravy, rozpoznávání řeči, překlad titulků i export běží přímo v telefonu.

Internet se používá jen ke stažení hlasového modelu (Hugging Face), modelů pro překlad titulků (Mozilla) a k nákupu přes App Store (Apple). Tyto servery vidí jen tvou IP adresu, jako u každé webové stránky. K fotkám má aplikace přístup jen k tomu, co sám vybereš, a do knihovny pouze ukládá.

Když nám napíšeš e-mail, použijeme tvou adresu a zprávu jen k odpovědi. Podle GDPR můžeš kdykoli požádat o jejich výmaz. Kontakt: bombocut.support@gmail.com, Kryštof Krechler, Praha.
