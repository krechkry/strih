## Czech word list (captions spelling, app/src/main/assets/cs-words.txt.gz)

49,756 Czech words used to turn Polish and Slovak spellings in captions into Czech ones: words seen at least 200 times
in Hermit Dave's FrequencyWords (2018 Czech list, built from OpenSubtitles 2018), Czech letters only.
- FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave: https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords (content CC BY-SA 4.0)
- Source corpus: OpenSubtitles 2018, http://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles2018.php
