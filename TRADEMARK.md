# Name, art and brand

The Bombocut **source code** is licensed under the GNU General Public License v3 (see `LICENSE`).

The **brand and art are not**. These stay © Krystof Krechler, all rights reserved, and are not covered by the GPL:

- The name **BOMBOCUT** / Bombocut and the "Made with BOMBOCUT" mark
- The app icon (`Bombocut/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset`)
- The meme cast and the green guy, as drawings, code-generated art and animations (`art/memes`, `Bombocut/Editor/MemeArt.swift` and the art it draws)
- The end card (`Bombocut/Resources/EndCard.mp4`), including the voice
- Store art and footage (`art/store`, `art/icon`)
- Voice recordings (`bench/audio`)

## Forks

You are welcome to fork, study and change the code under the GPL. If you publish a fork:

- give it a different name and icon,
- remove or replace the art listed above,
- don't present it as Bombocut or as made by Krystof Krechler.

## App Store

The official app is on the App Store as BOMBOCUT: one purchase, no subscription. Building it yourself is fine; buying it supports the work.
